BANKING — Active Recall MCQs

Based on Indian Economy Handbook, Chapter 11: Banking
Question 1
With reference to the evolution of Indian banking, consider the following statements:
1. The Bank of Hindustan was established before the Presidency Banks merged.
2. The three Presidency Banks merged to form the Imperial Bank of India in 1921.
3. The RBI was established before the Imperial Bank of India was formed.
Which are correct?
  • A. 1 and 2 only
  • B. 2 and 3 only
  • C. 1 and 3 only
  • D. 1, 2 and 3
Answer: A
Explanation: Statements 1 and 2 are supported by the notes. Imperial Bank was formed in 1921; RBI was established in 1935, so statement 3 is false.
Question 2
Which sequence is chronologically correct?
  • A. RBI established → Imperial Bank formed → SBI created → NABARD established
  • B. Imperial Bank formed → RBI established → SBI created → NABARD established
  • C. Imperial Bank formed → SBI created → RBI established → NABARD established
  • D. Bank of Hindustan → RBI established → Imperial Bank formed → SBI created
Answer: B
Explanation: Imperial Bank: 1921; RBI: 1935; SBI: 1955; NABARD: 1982.

Question 5

Question 40
A bank's total assets are ₹20,000 crore and net income is ₹600 crore. Its RoA is:
